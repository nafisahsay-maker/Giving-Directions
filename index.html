Direction Quest

A tourist is lost! Give directions in English to guide her to the right place.

Useful phrases
  • Go straight. / Go straight until the corner.
  • Turn left (right) on Malang Street.
  • Stop. It’s on your left (right).
  • It’s next to… / opposite… / across from… / between… and…
  • Go past the… / at the corner / at the intersection

Tip: keyboard works too. ↑ go straight, ← left, → right, Enter stop.
